# GSE146035 family metadata card

GSE146035 is the official GEO super-series for the rice root-tip study. Its
family SOFT record links four 3-day-old, 5-mm root-tip bulk RNA replicates
(GSE146033) and the two single-cell samples in GSE146034. The six samples are
indexed in ``GSE146035_sample_metadata.tsv`` and the original compressed SOFT
record is retained as ``GSE146035_family.soft.gz``.

This family record supplies sample provenance and bulk-RNA context. It does
not supply per-cell cell-type labels; the scRNA matrices remain an unlabeled
expression-pretraining asset until an author-provided or independently
validated annotation table is joined.

Official source: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE146035
